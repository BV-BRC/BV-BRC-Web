Synteny Graph (Panaconda)

Construct a pan-genome synteny graph from selected genomes or genome groups using context-guided protein family collinearity. Panaconda resolves repetitive gene families into syntenic contexts to uncover discrete comparative genomic and assembly phenomena: Superbubbles (bounded allelic/accessory cassettes), Copy Number Variants (tandem expansions & dispersed paralogs), Alternative Paths (genomic islands & HGT insertions), Structural Rearrangements (translocations & inversions), and Repeat-Induced Assembly Breaks & Candidate Scaffolds.
Tip: Select closely related genomes (e.g., within the same species or genus, up to ~400 well-conserved strains) for optimal syntenic resolution and interactive visualization. Job results include an interactive .gexf graph and 5 companion .tsv phenomenon reports.
