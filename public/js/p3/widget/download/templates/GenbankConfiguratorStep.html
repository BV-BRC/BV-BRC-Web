Contig Organization

Each contig is output as a separate GenBank record with its own LOCUS, FEATURES, and ORIGIN sections. This is the standard GenBank format for draft genomes with multiple contigs.

All contigs are concatenated into a single GenBank record per genome. Feature coordinates are adjusted to reflect positions in the merged sequence. Use this for genome visualization tools such as Artemis and SnapGene that expect a single LOCUS entry per genome.
